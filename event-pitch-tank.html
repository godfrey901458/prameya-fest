Pitch Tank — Rules & Regulations

RULESET / 01

Round 1 / Technique Round

  • Each team must consist of 2 members.
  • Choose one topic: Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR), Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), or Embedded Systems.
  • Prepare a PPT explaining the selected technology, techniques, and applications.
  • Bringing a laptop is mandatory.
  • Mobile phones may be used during preparation but not during the presentation.
RULESET / 02

Round 2 / Product Launch

  • Develop an innovative product using the selected technology.
  • Present its features, uniqueness, target customers, and marketing strategy.
  • Both team members must actively participate in the product launch.
  • Mobile phones are not allowed during the presentation.
RULESET / 03

General Rules

  • Creativity, innovation, and teamwork are encouraged.
  • The product must be relevant to the selected topic.
  • The judges’ decision will be final.
