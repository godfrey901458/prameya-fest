IT Quiz — Rules & Regulations

RULESET / 01

Round 1 / Preliminary

  • All teams will participate in this round.
  • Questions will cover Information Technology and Computer Science.
  • Teams must answer within the given time limit.
  • Top-scoring teams will qualify for Round 2.
RULESET / 02

Round 2 / Final

  • Qualified teams will compete in the final round.
  • Questions may cover technical knowledge, logical thinking, and general IT awareness.
  • The team with the highest score will be declared the winner.
RULESET / 03

General Rules

  • Each team must have 2 members.
  • Mobile phones and external help are not allowed.
  • Participants must maintain discipline.
  • The quizmaster’s decision will be final.
